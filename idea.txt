foxes are fearsome animals... when they smell yumyum food nearby nothing stops them from getting their treat.
you must get your freshly picked berries home, without a swarm of foxes trying to pick pocket you out of your
sweet sweet berries. Distract them, throw them bait, and don't let their cuteness fool you, they will steal
all your food. Be careful with the ones with a gun or a baseball bat.

SPECIFICATION
goal: get food form a to b
gameplay: through random generated forest
bring berries to goal. completion score depends
on how many you managed to deliver.

6 objects in game:
- spawn
- goal
- obstacle (tree)
- decor (grass)
- fox
- player

SPAWN: area, safehouse. fox repellent.
GOAL: your house. after unlocking door you get in.
OBSTACLE: static trees
DECOR: grass, skybox pretty stuff.
FOX: 3 types. normal, melee, ranged.
- normal: sniff, hear, alert, steal food. physicall block you.
- melee: not steal food but use you as a pinata

player: simple controller,
crouch,
sprint (cooldown),
movemeent disableable (lockpicking),
throw and collect decoy

simple ones body block
the ones with a gun shoot slow parryable bullets
the baseball ones see you as a pinata

the foxes make fox noises
they also do sniffings
